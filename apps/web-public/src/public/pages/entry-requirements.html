Entry Requirements

Everything you need to apply

Home Entry Requirements
What You Need

Requirements by Year Group

Nursery / EYFS (Ages 2–5)

  • Child must be 2 years old by September of entry year
  • Completed application form
  • Child's birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Immunisation records / health booklet
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Parent/Guardian valid ID
  • Informal play-based assessment visit

Primary School (Ages 6–11)

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school report (last 2 terms)
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Immunisation records
  • Two passport photographs
  • Parent/Guardian ID
  • Written entry assessment (English & Maths)
  • Parent interview

Secondary School (Ages 12–18)

  • Birth certificate
  • Last 2 years school reports
  • Transfer certificate
  • Common Entrance result (for JSS1)
  • BECE or JSCE result (for SS1)
  • Immunisation records
  • Two passport photographs
  • Written assessment & student interview

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my child join mid-term?

Yes, subject to availability of spaces. Contact our admissions office to discuss your situation.

What happens if my child fails the assessment?

We assess each child holistically. A disappointing assessment does not automatically mean rejection — we will discuss your child's needs and the best way forward.

Are there any special needs provisions?

We welcome children with mild learning differences. Please inform us during the application process so we can ensure we can adequately support your child.

How soon will I know if my child has been offered a place?

You will receive a decision within 5 working days of completing the assessment and interview.

Can I visit before applying?

Absolutely! We encourage all prospective families to book a school tour before applying.

Is there a waiting list?

Yes, for popular year groups. We recommend applying early to avoid disappointment, especially for Nursery and Primary 1.
