Home Primary School
Ages 6 – 11

Excellence Starts Here

Our Primary School (Primary 1 – Primary 6) provides a rigorous yet joyful educational experience for children aged 6 to 11. We follow a blended Nigerian and British curriculum that builds strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, sciences, and the arts.

Our primary years are designed to develop independent, creative, and confident learners who are well-prepared for the demands of secondary education and beyond.

Small class sizes ensure every child receives personalised attention and support from our experienced teaching staff.

Ages 6 – 11 Primary 1 – 6 Expert Teachers

Our Primary Curriculum

Literacy & English

Comprehensive reading, writing, grammar and communication skills built progressively from Primary 1 to 6. Our students develop a lifelong love for language.

Mathematics & Numeracy

From basic arithmetic to problem-solving and logical reasoning, our maths programme equips students with real-world mathematical confidence.

Science & Discovery

Hands-on experiments, observation, and inquiry-based learning in our well-equipped science lab develop critical thinking from an early age.

Arts, Culture & Values

Creative arts, music, cultural studies and Christian Religious Education develop the whole child — intellectually, spiritually and emotionally.

Why Choose Our Primary School?

Small Classes

Max 25 students per class ensures personalised attention and faster progress for every child.

Dual Curriculum

Nigerian and British curriculum combined gives students the best academic preparation available.

Strong Track Record

Our Common Entrance and internal assessment results consistently rank among the best in Abuja.

Pastoral Care

Dedicated form teachers monitor each child's emotional, social and academic wellbeing every day.
