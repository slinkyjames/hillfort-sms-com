Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS)

Ages 2–5 | Where learning begins with love

Home EYFS
Ages 2 – 5

A Nurturing Start to Every Child's Journey

Our Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) is designed for children aged 2 to 5 years. This is the most critical period of brain development, and we take that seriously — creating a warm, stimulating, and safe environment where children thrive.

Through structured play, hands-on activities, storytelling, music, and exploration, our qualified Early Years educators lay the strongest possible foundation for learning.

We follow the EYFS framework used in the UK, adapted for the Nigerian context, ensuring your child develops holistically across all seven areas of learning.

Ages 2 – 5 Small Class Sizes Nurturing Educators

7 Areas of Learning

Prime Areas

Personal, Social & Emotional Development

Building confidence, independence, and positive relationships.

Communication & Language

Listening, speaking, vocabulary, and early literacy skills.

Physical Development

Fine and gross motor skills, health awareness and coordination.

Specific Areas

Literacy

Reading, phonics, writing and early language comprehension.

Mathematics

Numbers, shapes, patterns, measuring and problem-solving.

Understanding the World

Science, technology, history, geography and community.

Expressive Arts & Design

Art, craft, music, drama and imaginative play.

A Typical Day in EYFS

7:30 – 8:00

Morning Arrival

Children arrive, settle in, free play and morning registration.

8:00 – 8:30

Morning Assembly

Songs, scripture, day's theme introduction and morning prayer.

8:30 – 10:00

Literacy & Numeracy

Phonics, reading, counting, shapes and structured learning activities.

10:00 – 10:30

Snack & Break

Healthy snack time, outdoor play and free exploration.

10:30 – 12:00

Creative & Discovery

Arts, crafts, science exploration and topic-based activities.

12:00 – 1:00

Lunch Time

Nutritious lunch, social skills, rest for younger children.

1:00 – 2:30

Themed Learning

Stories, music, movement, language development and roleplay.

2:30 – 3:00

Review & Dismissal

Daily recap, prayers, packing bags and afternoon dismissal.
