Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Hillfort International School ("we", "us", "the School") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all students, parents, staff and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information: Full names, dates of birth, contact information (phone, email, address), academic records and progress reports, health and medical information (where relevant), photographs and video footage taken at school events, and website usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal information is collected and used for the following purposes: Administering student admissions and enrolments, communicating with parents and guardians about school activities, academic reporting and progress monitoring, safeguarding and child protection obligations, compliance with government regulations and legal requirements, and improving our school services and website.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may share data with government bodies (such as the Ministry of Education), examination bodies (WAEC, NECO), and trusted service providers only where necessary and in compliance with applicable laws.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Our School Management System (SMS) is password-protected and access is restricted to authorised staff only.

5. Data Retention

We retain student records for a minimum of 7 years after a student leaves the school, in line with Nigerian educational regulations. Website usage data is retained for no longer than 12 months.

6. Your Rights

Under Nigeria's NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation), you have the right to access your personal data, request corrections, request deletion (subject to legal obligations), and withdraw consent for specific data processing activities. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@hillfortintlschool.ng.

7. Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies to improve user experience and analyse web traffic. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at: info@hillfortintlschool.ng or +234 (913) 026-7145.

Hillfort International School is a value-driven learning community in Abuja, Nigeria, committed to academic excellence and character development.
