About Hillfort

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

For over 15 years, Hillfort International School has been at the forefront of quality education in Nigeria. We combine academic excellence with character development, preparing students for success in an ever-changing world.

  • Government approved and internationally recognized
  • Holistic education approach (Academic, Social, Emotional)
  • Safe and nurturing learning environment
  • Strong parent-school partnership
