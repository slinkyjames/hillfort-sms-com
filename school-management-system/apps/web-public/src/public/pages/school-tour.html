Experience Hillfort in Person

A school tour is the best way to experience the Hillfort difference. Come and see our facilities, meet our teachers, and witness the warm, purposeful learning environment that makes us special.

Tour Duration

Approximately 45 – 60 minutes

Available Days

Monday – Friday (term time only)

Available Times

9:00am – 11:00am or 1:00pm – 3:00pm

Location

C225 Maccido Royal Estate, Galadimawa, FCT-Abuja

Your Tour Includes:

  • Guided campus walkthrough
  • Classroom observation (where possible)
  • Meet the head teacher
  • Q&A with admissions team
  • Information pack to take home
