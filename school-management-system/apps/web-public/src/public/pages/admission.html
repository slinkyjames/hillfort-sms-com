Admission Process

Your simple guide to joining the Hillfort family

Home Admission Process
How to Join

4 Simple Steps to Enrolment

01

Submit Application

Complete our online application form or collect a physical form from our school office. Provide all required information about your child and family.

Apply Online
02

Assessment & Interview

Your child will sit a brief age-appropriate assessment. Parents/guardians are also invited for a welcome interview with our admissions team.

03

Offer Letter

Successful applicants will receive an official offer of admission letter within 5 working days, outlining fee details and start date.

04

Acceptance & Enrolment

Confirm your place by paying the acceptance deposit. Collect your welcome pack, uniform, and prepare for your child's first day!

Important Information

When to Apply

Applications are accepted year-round. However, we recommend applying at least one term before your intended start date to secure your child's place. Our new academic year begins in September.

Documents Required

Birth certificate, previous school reports (if applicable), immunisation records, two passport photographs, and parent/guardian ID. See our Entry Requirements page for full details.

Application Fee

A non-refundable application processing fee is required upon submission. Contact our admissions office for the current fee schedule.

Class Availability

We maintain limited class sizes to ensure quality. Places fill quickly, especially for Nursery, Primary 1, and Year 7. Early application is strongly advised.
