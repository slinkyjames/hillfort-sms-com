Home Leadership Team
Meet the Team

Experienced Leaders, Passionate Educators

Our leadership team brings decades of combined experience in education, school management, and child development — all united by a passion for excellence.

Founder

Director Apostle (Mrs.) Agha Opia

Director & Founder

A visionary educator and leader with over 20 years of experience in school leadership and curriculum development, Director Apostle (Mrs.) Agha Opia founded Hillfort International School on the unwavering belief that every child deserves a world-class education deeply rooted in values. Her leadership has shaped a culture of excellence, warmth, and ambition that defines our school community.

Executive Leadership

Mr. E. Adeyemi

Executive Director

Mr. Adeyemi oversees the strategic direction, governance, and overall operations of Hillfort International School. With a strong background in institutional management, he works closely with the Director/Founder to drive the school's long-term vision, growth, and sustained academic excellence.

Senior Leadership

Mr. B. Okafor

Head Teacher (Primary)

Mr. Okafor leads our Primary School with 15 years of dedicated teaching experience. He is passionate about literacy, numeracy and building strong academic foundations that serve students throughout their education.

Mrs. C. Adamu

EYFS Coordinator

Specialising in early childhood education, Mrs. Adamu ensures our youngest learners receive the most nurturing and stimulating start to their school journey.

Ms. D. Bello

Admin & Admissions Officer

Ms. Bello is your first point of contact for all enquiries. She manages admissions, parent communications and school administration with warmth and efficiency.
