Our Approach

A Dual Curriculum for a Global Generation

At Hillfort, we believe children deserve the very best of both educational worlds. Our carefully integrated Nigerian and British curriculum prepares students for local examinations like WAEC and NECO, while simultaneously building the skills needed for Cambridge, A-Levels, and international university entry.

This dual approach ensures our graduates are competitive nationally and globally — academically equipped, critically thinking, and career-ready.

Nigerian Curriculum

  • Approved by FME & FCT Education Secretariat
  • Prepares for WAEC, NECO, JAMB
  • Strong focus on Nigerian history & civic values
  • Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa languages option
  • Nigerian cultural identity & heritage
  • Core subjects: Mathematics, English, Sciences, Social Studies

British Curriculum

  • Aligned to UK National Curriculum framework
  • Prepares for IGCSE and Cambridge pathway
  • Inquiry-based, project-led learning
  • Critical thinking & problem-solving emphasis
  • Strong literacy and numeracy foundations
  • STEM, Arts, Humanities integration
